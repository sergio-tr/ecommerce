## Integrity caveats and next completion unit

All source IDs resolve in the versioned historical/scoped registers, but a mapping to a broad policy ID **does not prove the requirement comes from that specific sentence or subsection**. A historical `PROPOSED — SELECTED` row does not authorize treating an entire capability as accepted. Differences between a source's historical selection and the later accepted M04 *scope classification* are not a contradiction: they are different decision axes.

1. For each CAP, retrieve the original source paragraph/location (DOCX section/paragraph or review document table row), compare it against that CAP's meaning and accepted M04 inclusion/exclusion/deferment.
