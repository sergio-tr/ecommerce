## Source fidelity and scope caveats

- Original 29 sourceId arrays were broad subsystem links. Directly matching selected historical clauses now exist for typed attributes (R0.2-07), inheritance (R0.3-02/03), demonstration tax (R0.3-08), MFA (R0.7-09), audit integrity (R0.7-19), cost (R0.8-19/R0.10-13) and open-source licensing (R0.1-08). These are candidates for source-link correction, not historical status updates.
- There is no verified original clause approving rich product asset storage, comprehensive catalog editor, inventory-correction dashboard or email provider as mandatory M04 features. Their proposed exclusion/deferment avoids inventing that approval.
- Original R0.1-08 explicitly chose an open-source public project with the *license undecided*; this is not settled merely because Apache-2.0 was a candidate.
- The user-approved W1-AP-01 scope obliges authoritative buyer status, safe guest access and financial reconciliation, but does not approve every user-interface arrangement or Keycloak deployment topology.
- For CAP-EGAME-007 and CAP-EGAME-009, further disjoint feature components are detailed in [COMPOSITE_CAPABILITIES.md](COMPOSITE_CAPABILITIES.md). CAP-PAY-012 is included there although it was not among the 29.
